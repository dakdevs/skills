## Interpretation

The traversal covers the complete local HIG navigation graph exposed by Apple at access time.
The operational package emphasizes shared foundations and the pages that materially affect Web
adaptation, native macOS, and iPhone. Pages specific to iPadOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS, games,
and optional Apple technologies remain inventoried for provenance but are not first-class routes.

## Freshness

HIG is living documentation. Reopen a page before relying on newly introduced materials,
component availability, current device specifications, or change-log-sensitive behavior.
