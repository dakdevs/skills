# Cross-Platform Review Gate

## Product Core

- [ ] The primary outcome is stated and visibly prioritized.
- [ ] Terminology, data meaning, action outcomes, and status meaning are stable across surfaces.
- [ ] Platform differences have a device, input, windowing, session, or familiarity rationale.
- [ ] Secondary capability does not obstruct the primary task.

## Agency and Recovery

- [ ] Guided flows can be skipped or exited unless mandatory for safety or setup.
- [ ] Back, cancel, close, undo, and restoration behave predictably.
- [ ] Recoverable errors preserve user-entered data and selection.
- [ ] Destructive actions are separated, specific, and never styled as the preferred default.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

- [ ] Meaning does not depend on color, sound, motion, hover, or gesture alone.
- [ ] Semantic names, roles, states, values, reading order, and focus order are correct.
- [ ] Text enlargement and longest localization reflow without loss of primary content or actions.
- [ ] Keyboard, pointer, touch, and assistive routes work as applicable.
- [ ] Light, dark, increased contrast, reduced motion, and RTL are tested where supported.

## Privacy and Responsibility

- [ ] Every collected datum is necessary to a user-facing capability.
- [ ] Permission requests occur in context with a truthful rationale.
- [ ] Denial and revocation do not block unrelated capability.
- [ ] Authentication and network interruption preserve work.
- [ ] Sharing/deletion scope is explicit, including other people or devices.

## States and Feedback

- [ ] Press, focus, selection, disabled, loading, success, and failure states exist as applicable.
- [ ] Loading, empty, offline, partial, error, denied, inactive, and restored states are intentional.
- [ ] Feedback is timely and proportional; failure gives a corrective next step.
- [ ] Empty states distinguish first use from filters or no results.

## Adaptability and Craft

- [ ] Layout survives the smallest and largest supported contexts.
- [ ] Hierarchy remains recognizable when columns or presentation change.
- [ ] Controls remain distinct from content over real backgrounds.
- [ ] Copy is concise, action-oriented, consistent, and platform-accurate.
- [ ] Motion explains change and remains comfortable under reduced-motion preferences.

## Completion Rule

Pass only when every applicable item has evidence. Mark nonapplicable items with a reason. Record
unverified items as remaining risk; do not silently treat them as passed.
