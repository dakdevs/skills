# Architecture

`SKILL.md` owns invocation and execution versus handoff routing. `references/workflow.md` owns the end-to-end implementation, evidence, general review, draft PR, and Claude monitoring sequence. `references/principles.md` owns inventory reconciliation, execution/verification routing, and cross-principle conflict rules. `references/ownership.md` owns model assignments, adversarial exchange, agreement, task state, and invalidation of sign-offs.

`references/principles/` preserves the complete installed principle instructions and supporting source/eval files as a portable baseline. These are reference documents, not nested installable skills. Local installed updates and additional principles are reconciled at invocation and frozen for the task. Every principle has two model owners, even for evidence-backed non-applicability. Owners advise and verify; implementation workers have explicit disjoint write ownership.

Investigation and baseline evidence precede agreed execution obligations. Apply obligations during implementation, then verify every principle and the two general review lenses with both models. Exchange counterarguments and require evidence-backed explicit agreement on the same snapshot. Implementation changes invalidate the entire final matrix. Draft publication and completed Claude review must cover the resulting final revision.

Never confuse unanimous assertions with proof, a task ID with a finished review, non-applicability with missing evidence, or a historical sign-off with coverage of new code. Disputes remain visible and block dependent completion; bounded exchanges prevent endless argument without new evidence.
